# User Guide - Ariestamed Launch

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Account Setup
- Sign up with your email
- Verify your email address
- Complete your profile

### 2. Add Your Digital Products

Navigate to **Products** and click "Add Product"

Fill in:
- **Product Name** - What you're selling
- **Description** - Key details about the product
- **Category** - course, ebook, software, membership, etc.
- **Price** - Product pricing
- **Features** - List of features (e.g., "30 hours of content", "Lifetime access")
- **Benefits** - Key benefits for your audience
- **Target Audience** - Who is this for?
- **Link** - Purchase/landing page URL
- **Image** - Product cover image

### 3. Connect Your Social Media Accounts

**Settings → Platform Credentials**

For each platform:
1. Click "Connect"
2. Follow the OAuth authorization
3. Authorize the app
4. Account will be connected

### 4. Create and Generate Content

#### Option A: AI Generate (Recommended)
1. Go to **Content → AI Generate**
2. Select your product
3. Choose content type: Caption, Post, Story, or Video Description
4. Select target platform
5. Choose tone: Professional, Casual, Humorous, Emotional
6. Click "Generate"
7. AI will create content in seconds
8. Review and approve

#### Option B: Manual Creation
1. Go to **Content → Create Manual**
2. Write your content
3. Add hashtags (comma-separated)
4. Add call-to-action
5. Save as Draft
6. Review and Approve

### 5. Schedule Posts

1. Go to **Posts → Schedule Post**
2. Select approved content
3. Choose platform(s)
4. Set date and time
5. Click "Schedule"

Posts automatically publish at scheduled time.

### 6. Monitor Analytics

**Analytics Dashboard** shows:
- Total views across all platforms
- Engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares)
- Platform-specific performance
- Post performance comparison

## 🎯 Best Practices

### Posting Schedule
- **Instagram**: 11 AM - 1 PM, 7 PM - 9 PM
- **Facebook**: Tue-Fri 1 PM - 3 PM
- **TikTok**: 6 AM - 10 AM, 7 PM - 11 PM
- **YouTube**: Consistent schedule (same time weekly)

### Content Tips

1. **Hook First** - Capture attention in first line
2. **Clear CTA** - Tell audience what to do ("Link in bio", "DM us", "Learn more")
3. **Platform-Specific** - Each platform has different best practices
4. **Hashtag Strategy** - Use 5-10 relevant, niche hashtags
5. **Engagement** - Ask questions, encourage comments

### AI Content Generation

**Tone Options:**
- **Professional** - Formal, authoritative
- **Casual** - Friendly, conversational
- **Humorous** - Funny, entertaining
- **Emotional** - Compelling, inspiring

**Content Types:**
- **Caption** - Short, social media posts
- **Post** - Longer form content
- **Story** - Short-form, ephemeral content
- **Video Description** - YouTube/TikTok descriptions

### Growing Your Audience

1. **Consistency** - Post regularly on a schedule
2. **Quality** - High-quality images/videos
3. **Engagement** - Respond to comments
4. **Niche** - Focus on your target audience
5. **Cross-Promote** - Share content across platforms

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Posts Not Publishing
- Check platform credentials are active
- Verify content is approved
- Check API rate limits
- Review error logs

### Analytics Not Updating
- Wait 24-48 hours for data to populate
- Manually refresh analytics
- Check platform API access

### AI Generation Issues
- Verify OpenAI API key is active
- Check account credits
- Try again with different parameters
